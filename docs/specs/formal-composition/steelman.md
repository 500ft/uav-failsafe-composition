# Steelman — the proposed thesis

## The position

Given individually specified failsafes, their *composition* can reach an unsafe state, and this can be decided automatically (a hybrid/timed model of PX4's failsafe logic plus the continuous quantities that drive its guards, checked for reachability) rather than found by testing; predicted interactions are confirmed in SITL (Study C), then replicated on ArduPilot and hardware (Study D). The bet: a predicted-and-confirmed interaction the fuzzing literature did not report is the pilot result.

## Aggregate verdict

**Position needs revision.** It wins on extractability for PX4 and on the shared apparatus; it is even on whether a target exists; it loses, as written, on distinctiveness (Avis unaddressed, formal axis unsearched) and on execution (no harness, no numeric kill criterion, ArduPilot not a repeat). None of these is fatal, and each has a concrete fix.

## The revised position

> For a completely configured PX4 vehicle, extract its failsafe logic as a timed automaton network with interval-abstracted continuous guards, validated against lockstep SITL at a preregistered agreement rate; decide four properties of the composed system (safety, deadlock freedom, priority consistency, bounded time-to-safe-state) with an existing checker; replay every witness in SITL through the repo's own harness; report predicted-and-confirmed interactions, predicted-but-refuted ones, and the verified-null case as equally publishable outcomes. Distinguish from Avis (execution-based invariant checking) by method and by the witness-replay artifact. ArduPilot and hardware are a second, funded extraction and replication, not a repeat. The empirical recovery-contract measurements (RQ1–RQ2) become Study A's guard calibration and the fallback thesis; RQ3–RQ4 move to trigger-gated scope.

## Bottom line

The formal-composition thesis is the stronger spine than the tube thesis, because it can produce a decidable, replayable, externally checkable artifact from open code with the apparatus this repo already specifies. It does not hold *as written*: it ignores the closest prior art (Avis), has no searched novelty axis, no operational definition of "unsafe", no numeric kill criterion, and depends on a harness that does not exist. Fix those five things, keep the empirical measurements as the calibration layer, and commit.
